1. Who we are

Aaspass is a hyperlocal shopping app that lets you discover nearby shops and order products for delivery. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Aaspass Android app and how we use and protect it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: mobile number (used for OTP login), and optionally your name, email address, date of birth and profile photo.
  • Delivery addresses you save in the app.
  • Location: your approximate or precise device location, only when you allow it, to show shops and offers near you and to calculate delivery.
  • Orders and payments: items ordered, order status, delivery details and payment status. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment partner and are never stored by us.
  • App activity: cart, wishlist, ratings, feedback and support requests.
  • Device information: device type and a push-notification token so we can send order updates.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and log you in via OTP.
  • To show nearby shops, products and offers, and to place, deliver and track your orders.
  • To process payments and refunds through our payment partner.
  • To send order status updates and, with your consent, offers and notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time.
  • To provide customer support and to improve the app.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations, including tax and accounting rules.

4. Who we share it with

  • Sellers on Aaspass receive the details needed to fulfil your order: your name, delivery address, mobile number and order items.
  • Payment gateway (such as Razorpay) to process online payments.
  • SMS and push notification providers (such as MSG91 and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver OTPs and order updates.
  • Cloud hosting and storage providers (such as Amazon Web Services) where our servers and uploaded images are hosted.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. Order and payment records are retained for as long as required by tax, accounting and consumer-protection laws, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your Aaspass account at any time from My Profile > Delete account inside the app, or without the app at backend.aaspass.net/delete-account. Your account is scheduled for deletion immediately with a 7-day grace period during which logging in restores it. After 7 days your personal data, addresses, cart and wishlist are permanently deleted, and any order records we must keep by law are anonymised so they are no longer linked to you.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored on access-controlled servers. Login uses one-time passwords instead of stored passwords.

8. Children

Aaspass is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date of the last update shown at the top.

10. Contact us

For any questions about this policy or your data, contact us through the Help & Support section in the Aaspass app.
